Privacy Policy for GRIDLOCK

GRIDLOCK does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

No accounts, analytics, advertising IDs, or tracking technologies are used.

Any game progress is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted off the device.

If you contact the developer, your email will only be used to respond to your inquiry.
